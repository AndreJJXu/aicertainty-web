Junjie Xu

Junjie Xu is a Direct Ph.D. researcher at East China Normal University, School of Computer Science and Technology, Shanghai, working on multimodal affective computing and generative systems.

Research statement
“I want to place cross-modal alignment, affective understanding, and controllable generation on the same research path.”

His publication record spans multimodal generation, affective AI, and language understanding: 14 first-author papers among 27 publications. Two ECNU research projects led, one rated Excellent at completion. 14 published Chinese patent applications as co-inventor — publication numbers shown for verification.

His recent work turns to evidence and evaluation fidelity: auditing whether LLM safety judgments follow expressed evidence or severity cues (Evidence Before Severity, Under review · AAAI 2027), and a preregistered agent-safety study that published its own null (ANI — 6,480 confirmatory episodes, NONE_CONFIRMED).

From November 2026 to November 2027, Junjie Xu will be a CSC-funded visiting researcher at Nanyang Technological University, hosted by Prof. Eric Cambria. The placement is personal academic funding for the founder; it is not an affiliation, endorsement, or partnership of AI Certainty.

Student awards include the 2025 Kuanrui Talent Scholarship and the 2023 Shanghai Outstanding Graduate award.
