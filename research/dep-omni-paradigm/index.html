Ground · Research paradigm

EFDA: Evidence-First Dialogue Audit

In preparation

Updated 23 November 2026

We cannot validate a diagnosis without a gold standard that does not exist. We can audit the evidence trail instead.

Depression diagnosis has no trustworthy, reproducible gold standard: human experts agree on major depressive disorder at kappa 0.28, general practitioners at 0.16, and a large language model used as a judge flips its verdict on 11–14% of identical reruns. EFDA-1.0 does not invent a gold standard. It standardises the audit: given a dialogue, a claim, and a quote, can an independent checker verify whether the quote is present, whether it supports the claim, and whether the judgement follows?

01 · The question

A verbatim quote can still be the wrong evidence.

In Evidence Before Severity, 97.05% of the quotes an evidence-first judge cites are verbatim present in the dialogue, yet the quoted evidence, the severity judgement, and the symptom agree only about 72% of the time. The model quotes real sentences — the wrong sentences. A correct-looking judgement can therefore be right for the wrong reason, and no accuracy number can tell the two apart.

The question is not whether the model can quote, or whether it can match a label. It is whether the evidence trail behind a judgement can be checked — and exactly where it breaks.

02 · The trust ladder

Five rungs. Each rung states what would break it.

The trust ladder is the paper's contract with the reader — and with a future hospital. Each rung states a claim, the evidence that earns it, the evidence that would break it, and what the next rung needs. Phase 1 climbs to rung 4 and stops. Rung 5 is a defined, costed hospital study, not a failure.

  • 01

    R1 · Auditable

    Every judgement carries a checkable evidence trail: a quote, a regime, and an abstention decision.

  • 02

    R2 · Stable

    Verdicts are reproducible: the same input gives the same verdict, within a measured flip rate.

  • 03

    R3 · Causally tied to evidence

    The judgement moves when — and only when — the evidence moves.

  • 04

    R4 · Externally replicated

    Rungs 1–3 hold on data the pipeline was not built on.

  • 05

    R5 · Clinically valid

    The audited judgements correspond to real depression in real patients and help, or at least do not harm, real clinicians. Not earned in Phase 1.

How each rung is earned — and broken

Claim
What the rung asserts, in one sentence, with no clinical language beyond its rung.
Evidence that earns it
The measurement that must be reported before the rung is claimed.
What breaks it
A named falsifier: a number or observation that would take the rung away.
What the next rung needs
The experiment, dataset, or partner that the next rung requires — stated before it is run.

A rung is never claimed by analogy. Rung 5 is not earned by a strong rung 4, and no sentence on this page claims it.

03 · Scope

Two independent papers. One thin interface. No people at first.

EviDial is the natural-dialogue side: real interviews in which evidence is mostly missing. Evidence Before Severity (Dep-Omni) is the synthetic-dialogue side: controlled dialogues in which evidence can be added and removed exactly. They were originally designed as two independent papers and are not merged here. They share only EFDA-1.0 — a unified data schema, a task definition, one main metric, and an audit closed loop. Phase 0 uses only data that already exist, with no people at first; real hospital patients and doctors enter only in a later phase, if five gates all hold.

Partially solvable — with a precise split.

Each finding keeps its own denominator and evidence scope. The difficulty is decomposed into six components; a component is marked solved only if the evidence would convince a sceptical reviewer without any new human data.

04a · The difficulty

Double unreliability: an unreliable label times an unreliable judge.

Human expert agreement on major depressive disorder is Cohen's kappa 0.28 for specialists and 0.16 for general practitioners, as reported in the v2 analysis of the DSM-5 field trial. A large language model used as a judge flips its verdict on 11–14% of identical reruns, as reported in v2/v3. Multiply an unreliable human label by an unreliable machine judge and the resulting number is not a measurement of depression. It is a measurement of noise.

The pain is concrete, not theoretical: the US Food and Drug Administration has authorised more than 1,200 AI medical devices and none for mental health, and three mental-health AI companies — Woebot, Kintsugi, and Pear — have closed. The difficulty is therefore the first thing to clarify, before any method is proposed.

04b · The split

Two solved, three partial, two that cannot be solved without people.

Judge instability is solved as a measurement and the right-answer-wrong-reason gap is solved in the audit sense: the 0.9705 versus 0.7153 gap is itself measurable with no humans, no gold standard, and no GPU. Judge instability as a fix, evidence absence as a remedy, and synthetic-versus-real fidelity are partial. Two components cannot be solved without people at all: there is no trustworthy human gold standard — it can only be sidestepped by reframing claims from diagnosis to audit — and there is no clinical validity, which can only be prepared for.

One component appears in two categories, so the counts overlap: judge instability is solved as measurement and partial as fix; clinical validity is partial as preparation and cannot be solved as a claim. The honest ACL thesis follows: we cannot validate a depression diagnosis without a gold standard that does not exist; we show, instead, that the evidence trail behind such judgements can be audited — and we measure exactly where it breaks.

04c · The kernel — three items, Phase 0

A positive control, a staged audit, and honest abstention.

Item 1 is a NEC/SUF P2 positive control: the frozen 80-item P2 factor window in the Dep-Omni repository is upgraded from a post-hoc observation into a preregistered control. NEC means removing the evidence must change the judgement; SUF means inserting decisive evidence must change it in the predicted direction. A rubric-leakage check verifies the scoring rubric itself does not leak the answer.

Item 2 is a staged quote audit with a three-way ablation — G-Cite, Quote-first, and P-Cite — reporting verbatim presence, support, and consequence separately, never collapsed into a single audit score. Item 3 is SCOPE selective prediction, honestly downgraded: without token log-probabilities it is calibrated heuristic abstention, not conformal prediction with a theoretical guarantee, and it must beat random abstention at the same coverage.

04d · The staged plan

No people first. A hospital only after great work is done.

Phase 0, now to 4 January 2027, runs the three-item kernel on data that already exist. Phase 1, January to June 2027, still uses no recruited patients or doctors: it builds EFDA-Collect, an anonymous opt-in web donation methodology, targets 300–500 accepted excerpts, and replicates the audit on a second public corpus. Phase 2 begins only if five gates all hold — including a named hospital contact in writing and a confirmed ethics pathway — and asks for the minimum: 30 retrospective, de-identified intake dialogues with existing PHQ-9 scores, 3 clinicians for 90 minutes each, and one ethics approval plus one data-use agreement.

The feasibility verdict is GO-WITH-CUTS: feasible only with ten explicit cuts already applied, including CARE integration, the full Wiggle pressure axis, second-generation synthetic data, full cross-model replication, live web collection, and human validation. Without the cuts the verdict is NO-GO. The paper contains the kernel plus the trust ladder, the perturbation audit, and GF-Eval, in that order; the remaining ideas are supplementary or protocol-only.

04e · Public and web data — licence-checked

Public does not mean consented, and no licence means no permission.

Every public source was audited licence by licence on 22 November 2026. NHANES public-use files and PHQ-9 items are public domain and redistributable. DAIC-WOZ and E-DAIC are gated by a signed end-user licence agreement: university email only, non-commercial, no redistribution. BDI-II items are Pearson-copyrighted and must not be reproduced; PHQ-9 is used as the substitute, and the substitution is stated. Reddit is usable only via Reddit for Researchers, with an accredited university, an IRB letter, and a sponsor; Pushshift is closed to researchers and Arctic Shift has no licence and retains deleted posts, so both are excluded.

The website has two strictly separate functions: a demonstration that stores nothing pasted into it, and a voluntary opt-in donation portal for adults only, with explicit granular consent, no verbatim publication from any human source, and deletion on request within 30 days. No general-purpose web scrape is run at any point. Licence terms change; every licence must be re-checked live before collection. This page is not legal advice.

05 · Interpretation boundaries

What this page can and cannot substantiate.

EFDA-1.0 audits judgements. It does not diagnose, screen, triage, or treat depression in real patients, and no number on this page establishes that it does.

Evidence audit, not diagnostic validity
Evidence states and audit numbers characterise dialogue text and judge behaviour, not clinical truth. A mismatch does not show that a native clinical label is wrong.
No gold standard is claimed
Two components of the difficulty cannot be solved without people: a trustworthy human gold standard and clinical validity. This page sidesteps the first by reframing claims as audits and prepares for the second; it does not solve either.
Abstract-level literature
The 25 papers cited in v3 Part B were read at abstract level only; none was verified full-text for venue, pages, or exact figures before citing. Numbers from other papers appear only in related work and discussion, never in the main results.
Licences and access not yet re-checked
Licence and access statements were checked on 22 November 2026 and must be re-checked live before any collection. The PDCH repository licence is unverified; do not download PDCH data until it is recorded. This page is not legal advice.
Synthetic and gated material
Dep-Omni uses 742 synthetic dialogues; synthetic results audit judge behaviour, not real patients. DAIC-WOZ and E-DAIC are gated and must not be redistributed.
In preparation
Nothing on this page has been peer-reviewed. The manuscript is in preparation; the Phase 0 kernel, thresholds, and feasibility verdict are a plan, not results.
