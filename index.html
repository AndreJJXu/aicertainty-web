01 / The human dimensionHuman × intelligence

A person is more
than a prompt.

Attention. Effort.
Affect. Context.

These belong in the conversation about intelligent systems.

We aim to build the foundations for AI that can account for human state, adapt within clear boundaries, and verify whether its response helped.

Understanding begins with measurement. Responsibility begins with knowing where the evidence stops.

02 / Current focus

Current work

Make corrections
carry forward.

Corrections & adaptation

When someone corrects an AI system, or their needs change, what should the system do next?

In design

We are designing studies of repeated, real use. No experiments have been run yet.

The research question ↗

Questions

  1. Which situations does a correction apply to?
  2. How long should it last?
  3. When is one more question worth asking?

What we will measure

  • Repeated mistakes
  • Repeated explanations
  • Whether the task gets done

Compared with strong baselines, in tasks people return to.

01 / Ground↗

EviDial

A benchmark that separates agreement with depression symptom labels from the textual evidence available to support an assessment.

Research manuscript

Not a reviewed result.

BoundaryEvidence support, not diagnostic validity. Candidate references and an exploratory development audit do not establish model rankings.

02 / Verify↗

ANI

Affective Non-Interference studies how input construction, agent behavior, and scoring affect conclusions about emotional expression at authorization boundaries.

Research manuscript

Not a reviewed result.

BoundaryConditional findings from synthetic tasks and saved records; clean affect-induced expansion of an unchanged authorized action set remains undemonstrated.

03 / Measure↗

I'm Fine

A study of how LLM self-reports and a fixed observer differ in score levels, model ordering, and temporal variation across adapted affective instruments.

Research manuscript

Not a reviewed result.

BoundaryDescriptive results from archived model interactions. No clinical, subjective-experience, or single-mechanism causal conclusions.

04 / How we think

Our principles ↗

Measure the state.
Respect the person.

Human-defined outcomes guide the system. A signal can support an inference; it never defines a person's value.

Long-term development loop

  1. 01→

    Sense

    Name the signal and its source.

  2. 02→

    Model

    State the construct being inferred.

  3. 03→

    Calibrate

    Preserve context and uncertainty.

  4. 04→

    Adapt

    Respond within the declared boundary.

  5. 05↺

    Verify

    Check the human-defined outcome.
