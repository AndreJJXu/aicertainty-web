01 / Ground↗

EviDial

A benchmark that separates agreement with depression symptom labels from the textual evidence available to support an assessment.

Submitted · ICLR 2027

A status, not a result.

BoundaryEvidence support, not diagnostic validity. Candidate references and an exploratory development audit do not establish model rankings.

02 / Verify↗

ANI

Affective Non-Interference studies how input construction, agent behavior, and scoring affect conclusions about emotional expression at authorization boundaries.

Submitted · ICLR

BoundaryConditional findings from synthetic tasks and saved records; clean affect-induced expansion of an unchanged authorized action set remains undemonstrated.

03 / Measure↗

I'm Fine

A study of how LLM self-reports and a fixed observer differ in score levels, model ordering, and temporal variation across adapted affective instruments.

Submitted · ICLR

BoundaryDescriptive results from archived model interactions. No clinical, subjective-experience, or single-mechanism causal conclusions.

04 / How we think

Our principles ↗

Measure the state.
Respect the person.

Human-defined outcomes guide the system. A signal can support an inference; it never defines a person's value.

Long-term development loop

  1. 01→

    Sense

    Name the signal and its source.

  2. 02→

    Model

    State the construct being inferred.

  3. 03→

    Calibrate

    Preserve context and uncertainty.

  4. 04→

    Adapt

    Respond within the declared boundary.

  5. 05↺

    Verify

    Check the human-defined outcome.
